Jeju: a falling trend with several changes at once

  • Shelter intake fell from 7,767 (2019) to 3,886 (2024).
  • Yard-dog neutering counted 747 (2022), 944 (2023) and 481 (2024), 2,172 in all.
  • Over the same years: a registration fee waiver, 67 registration clinics and 74.0% microchip implantation against 51.8% nationally.
  • The province credits neutering. There is no comparison area, and intake counts include lost animals.

Source: DailyVet, 12 July 2025 and 6 May 2021. The 2019 figure appears as both 7,767 and 7,734.

Flags on existing material

  • DR22 says the 2023 reasons for not neutering allowed several answers, but the rows sum to 97.2%. The 2022 rows sum to 134.1%. Check Table 95 before using the fall in "fear of pain" from 40.5% to 21.8%.
  • DR22's "dogs tied up outdoors neutered" (32.6% in 2022, 43.2% in 2023) and Aware 2025's "outdoor dogs" (54.1%) are different groupings from different surveys. They are not one rising line.
  • The Paju document gives two sets of figures for shelter intake (241 or 252) and euthanasia (127 or 129).
  • The audit's "64,000 a year" is a whole-period figure. The pace still needed after August 2024 is higher.
  • The free acquaintance split by housing type (47.2% detached, 33.9% apartments) is from Aware 2025 Table 47, covering all pets.
